Behind every voice.

Voice Auditions is a collection of locally synthesized demonstrations. The audio is generated, not a collection of recordings of real performers. We do not claim endorsement by model authors, dataset contributors, or the creators of the original turbo-encabulator film.

The production tools

  • Kokoro-82M, created by hexgrad: open weights under Apache-2.0. The upstream voice catalog supplies preset language and gender labels.
  • MLX-community's Kokoro conversion and voice tensors provide the locally cached model assets.
  • MLX-Audio and MLX provide local Apple Silicon synthesis; both are MIT-licensed.
  • Misaki supplies text-to-phoneme processing under Apache-2.0. FFmpeg encodes the web recordings. No model weights or tool binaries are redistributed here.
  • Typography: Playfair Display and DM Sans, served through Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. System fonts are used if unavailable. Loading those fonts makes a request to Google Fonts; the site does not include analytics or tracking scripts.

Voice-source acknowledgments

The upstream catalog credits these source datasets for particular presets. Those acknowledgments are retained here; the catalog's model labels do not identify the voice as any specific person.

  • jf_gongitsune: Gongitsune, Koniwa source reference.
  • jf_nezumi: Nezumi no Yomeiri, Koniwa source reference.
  • jf_tebukuro: Tebukuro wo Kai ni, Koniwa source reference.
  • jm_kumo: Kumo no Ito, Koniwa source reference.
  • ff_siwis: The SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database, University of Edinburgh DataShare.

See the upstream CC BY links for the source-specific attribution and license details.

A very old, very good joke

John Hellins Quick's 1944 engineering satire started the turbo-encabulator tradition. Bud Haggart's performance, directed by Dave Rondot and photographed by John Choate in the late 1970s, brought it to film. Watch the original filmed version or read the director's account.

This project's audition passage is a new homage. It is not a transcription, and the original film is linked rather than redistributed.

Reproduction and evidence

The repository includes the source catalog, recording scripts, install instructions, and per-file hashes. Recording measurements document file integrity and production settings. They do not certify human listening quality.
